The person behind the shelf

Hi, I'm Cassie.

I believe the best camera is the one you actually want to take with you. After years of shooting weddings, road trips, and whatever looked interesting on the walk home, I started collecting the gear that made those days easier.

Now I pass those pieces along. Every item gets a hands-on check, a clear condition note, and a little context about where it shines. No pressure, no jargon wall, no pretending the newest is always the best.
